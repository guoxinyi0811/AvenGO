Leg Press

Plan: 3 sets × 8–12 reps

Movement guide
Equipment setupAdjust the seat so the knees reach about 90° at the bottom. Select the displayed load and check the safety handles before starting.
Start positionPlace both feet mid-to-high on the platform, hip-width apart, with toes and knees pointing in the same direction. Keep your back and hips on the pad.
BreathingInhale as the platform lowers. Exhale as you press.
TempoLower for 2–3 seconds → no pause → press in 1–2 seconds.
Force orderBrace first → lower under control → press through the whole foot → stop before the knees lock.
Right feelingThighs and glutes work while the lower back stays against the pad.
Common errorsIf the lower back lifts, reduce depth. Keep the knees tracking with the toes and avoid locking them.
