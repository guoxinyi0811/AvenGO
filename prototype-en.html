Goblet Squat

Plan: 3 sets × 10 reps · start at 10 lb

Movement guide
SetupStand slightly wider than hip-width. Turn toes out 10–20°. Track knees over the second and third toes. Hold one dumbbell vertically at chest height; keep your spine long and gaze forward and slightly down.
BreathingInhale and brace before descending. Exhale as you drive up through the floor.
TempoLower for 2–3 seconds → no pause at the bottom → stand in about 1 second.
Force orderBrace first → bend knees and hips together → push the floor away with the whole foot → finish by standing tall.
Right feelingPressure stays across the whole foot. Thighs and glutes do most of the work; the lower back stays quiet.
Common errorsIf knees cave in, reduce depth and guide them toward the toes. If heels lift, widen the stance slightly or reduce depth.
